HONEST SCOPE

What it is, and isn’t

This is an original, local teaching prototype inspired by transformation-tracking research. The scene and video are generated; the code uses handcrafted shapes and simple mask-overlap rules.

  • No real camera frames or trained segmentation model
  • No point sampler, mask decoder, or MLLM judge
  • No VISTA benchmark or reported research performance
  • Track IDs are explanatory labels, not learned identities
