HONEST SCOPE

What it is, and isn’t

This original local teaching prototype pairs real watch-repair footage with a separate generated scene. Handcrafted shapes and simple mask-overlap rules apply only to the synthetic scene.

  • No object tracking or segmentation on the real repair clip
  • No point sampler, mask decoder, or MLLM judge
  • No VISTA benchmark or reported research performance
  • Track IDs are explanatory labels, not learned identities
